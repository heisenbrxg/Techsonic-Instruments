Our modular conveyor lines are engineered for smooth, continuous material movement across your production floor. Designed for manufacturing, assembly, and packaging operations, these systems deliver reliable performance while protecting sensitive electronic components.

Key Features

• Modular Conveyor Lines: Smooth, continuous material movement for optimal workflow
• Durable Heavy-Duty Construction: Ensuring long, reliable operation in demanding environments
• ESD-Safe Conveyor Options: Protect sensitive electronic components from static discharge
• Adjustable Speed Control: Flexible and efficient workflows tailored to your needs
• Integrated Automation and Sensors: Reducing manual handling and improving accuracy
• Customizable Layouts: Designed to fit any production floor configuration
• Seamless Integration: Works with inspection, rework, packaging, and assembly zones

Applications

Our conveyor lines are ideal for various industries including electronics manufacturing, automotive assembly, food processing, pharmaceutical packaging, and general industrial applications. They can handle a wide range of products from small components to larger assemblies.

"Streamline your production with our reliable conveyor systems. Designed for efficiency, built for durability."

Technical Specifications

• Belt widths available: 200mm to 1000mm
• Speed range: 5-30 meters per minute
• Load capacity: Up to 50kg per meter
• Power supply: 220V/380V AC
• Frame material: Aluminum alloy or stainless steel
• Control options: Manual, semi-automatic, or fully automated

With years of experience in industrial automation, we provide conveyor solutions that are tailored to your specific needs. Our systems are backed by comprehensive technical support, installation assistance, and after-sales service to ensure optimal performance throughout the product lifecycle.
