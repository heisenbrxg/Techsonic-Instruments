Control & Instrumentation Laboratory Equipment

Our comprehensive Control & Instrumentation Laboratory provides state-of-the-art equipment for studying control systems, measurement techniques, and industrial instrumentation. This lab is essential for electrical and instrumentation engineering students to gain practical knowledge in process control, motor control, and measurement systems.

Complete Equipment List (21 Items)

1. PID Controller
Advanced PID controller trainer for studying proportional, integral, and derivative control actions with real-time tuning capabilities.

2. AC/DC Motor Speed Control (Transfer Function Study) Trainer
Comprehensive trainer for analyzing motor speed control systems and transfer function characteristics.

3. Design of Lead-Lag Compensation
Control system compensation trainer for studying lead-lag compensator design and implementation.

4. AC/DC Motor Position Control System
Precision position control trainer with feedback mechanisms for accurate positioning applications.

5. Modelling of Systems Machines, Sensors and Transducers
Comprehensive system modeling kit for understanding dynamic behavior of various systems.

6. Stability Analysis of Linear System (Stable/Unstable)
Control system stability analysis trainer with Routh-Hurwitz and Nyquist criteria implementation.

7. Synchro Transmitter and Receiver
Synchro system trainer for studying angular position transmission and reception.

8. AC/DC Bridges (Wheatstone, Kelvin, Anderson, Schering, etc.)
Complete bridge measurement system for resistance, inductance, and capacitance measurements.

9. Pressure Measurement Trainer
Industrial pressure measurement system with various pressure sensors and transmitters.

10. Temperature Measurement using RTD, Thermistor
Temperature measurement trainer with RTD and thermistor sensors for precision temperature control.

11. Displacement Measurement Trainer using LVDT
Linear Variable Differential Transformer based displacement measurement system.

12. Optical Measurement Trainer using LDR, Photodiode
Light intensity measurement system with various optical sensors.

13. Strain Measurement Trainer
Strain gauge based measurement system for stress and strain analysis.

14. Flow Measurement Trainer
Flow measurement system with various flow sensors and transmitters.

15. Power and Energy Measurement Trainer
Comprehensive power measurement system for single and three-phase systems.

16. Instrumentation Amplifier Trainer
Signal conditioning and amplification trainer for instrumentation applications.

17. A/D and D/A Converter Module
Analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog conversion trainer for data acquisition systems.

18. Stepper Motor Position Control Trainer
Precision stepper motor control system for accurate positioning applications.

19. Stepper Motor and Drive Study Trainer
Complete stepper motor drive system for studying motor characteristics and control.

20. Servo Motor Study Trainer
Servo motor control system with feedback for high-precision applications.

21. AC/DC Motor and Drive Study Trainer
Comprehensive motor drive trainer for studying various motor control techniques.
