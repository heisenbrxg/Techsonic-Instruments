Digital Torque Meter - Precision Torque Measurement

Our digital torque meters are essential tools for measuring and testing torque of electric screwdrivers, wrenches, and other torque-controlled tools. Designed for quality control and calibration in manufacturing environments, these meters deliver accurate, reliable measurements.

Key Features

• Bidirectional Measurement: Measures torque in both clockwise and counterclockwise directions
• Multiple Measurement Modes: TRACK, PEAK, and FIRST PEAK modes for versatile testing
• High Accuracy: Typically within ± (F.S.) for precise measurements
• Digital Display: Clear LCD readout with backlight for easy reading
• Data Storage: Memory function to store measurement results
• Multiple Units: Display in N·m, kgf·cm, lbf·in, and other units

Applications

Perfect for testing electric screwdrivers, pneumatic wrenches, torque wrenches, and other torque tools. Widely used in electronics manufacturing, automotive assembly, aerospace, and quality control laboratories for tool calibration and verification.

"Ensure proper torque application with our precision digital torque meters. Accurate measurements for quality assurance."

Technical Specifications

• Torque range: 0.15-10 N·m (model dependent)
• Accuracy: ±0.5% F.S.
• Resolution: 0.001 N·m
• Sampling rate: 1000 Hz
• Display: LCD with backlight
• Power: Rechargeable Li-ion battery
• Operating temperature: 0°C to 40°C

Why Choose Our Digital Torque Meter?

Our torque meters combine precision engineering with user-friendly operation. With multiple measurement modes and high accuracy, they provide reliable data for quality control and tool calibration. The robust construction ensures long-term reliability in demanding industrial environments.
