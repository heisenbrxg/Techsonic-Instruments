PCB Separator - Precision Board Cutting Solution

Our PCB separator machines provide precise, stress-free cutting of printed circuit boards. Designed for electronics manufacturing, these machines ensure clean separation without damaging components or creating harmful stress on solder joints.

Key Features

• Dual-Blade System: Separates V-groove and SMT boards without damaging components
• Adjustable Cutting Speed: Variable speed control for different PCB materials
• Adjustable Pressure: Precise pressure control prevents component damage
• Speed & Durability: Designed for high-volume production environments
• Emergency Stop Function: Operator safety with instant shutdown capability
• Clean Cutting: Minimal dust generation with integrated vacuum system

Applications

Ideal for electronics manufacturing, PCB assembly lines, prototyping labs, and repair centers. Perfect for separating panel PCBs, LED strips, and multi-board assemblies with precision and efficiency.

"Achieve perfect PCB separation every time. Our machines deliver precision cutting without compromising board integrity."

Technical Specifications

• Cutting method: Linear blade or V-groove cutting
• PCB thickness: 0.6mm - 3.2mm
• Cutting speed: Adjustable, up to 100mm/s
• Cutting length: Up to 350mm
• Power supply: 220V AC, 50/60Hz
• Dimensions: 450mm × 350mm × 200mm
• Weight: Approximately 25kg

Why Choose Our PCB Separator?

Our PCB separators are engineered for reliability and precision. With advanced cutting technology and robust construction, they deliver consistent results while protecting your valuable PCB assemblies from damage. Backed by comprehensive support and training.
